The Root Cause

What's Keeping You Awake

Your body is ready. Your mind isn’t.

You’re exhausted, but the moment your head hits the pillow, your brain starts replaying, planning, worrying, and solving.

You’re trying to solve tomorrow at 1 AM.

The thought feels urgent. The problem probably isn’t. But your brain hasn’t learned that bedtime is not the time to deal with it.

The harder you try to sleep, the harder it becomes.

Checking the time. Forcing relaxation. Thinking about how little sleep you’ll get. The pressure itself keeps you awake.

Your bed has become part of the problem.

After enough restless nights, your brain can start associating bed with frustration and wakefulness instead of sleep.

Not general relaxation, not sleep tracking — a structured, clinically-grounded path built specifically for the "exhausted but wired" pattern.
