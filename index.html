That’s why things like cutting coffee, and meditation aren’t enough. That’s why MindVeda solves your sleep problems at the root.

The 21-night reset

How MindVeda actually works

Three stages, delivered night by night — never dumped on you all at once.

01

Take the 5-minute screening

A short set of questions on your sleep, stress and thought patterns — so night one is written for your specific kind of "wired."

02

We specialise in anxiety-driven insomnia

Not general relaxation, not sleep tracking — a structured, clinically-grounded path built specifically for the "exhausted but wired" pattern.
