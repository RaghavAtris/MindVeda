The Root Cause

What's Actually Keeping You Awake

Insomnia is often a symptom of deeper challenges like anxiety, stress, or other things going on in your life.

That’s why things like cutting coffee, melatonin gummies and meditation aren’t enough. MindVeda solves your sleep problems at the root.

The 21-night reset

How MindVeda actually works

Three stages, delivered night by night — never dumped on you all at once.

01

Take the 5-minute screening

A short set of questions on your sleep, stress and thought patterns — so night one is written for your specific kind of "wired."

02

Get your night-by-night program

One message, one exercise, every evening for 21 nights — a short audio, a reframing prompt, or a wind-down ritual. Ten minutes, no more.

03

Wake up without the 3am spiral

By the final week, you're not fighting your mind at night — you've built the habits that keep it quiet on its own.

Built for Indian nights, not translated from one

Written for the 11pm-to-1am stretch that Indian professionals actually live in — deadline anxiety, joint-family expectations, and a phone that never fully switches off. Not a Western sleep app with the currency changed.

Inside the program

The techniques we actually use

Adapted from Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) — the approach sleep clinics reach for first, before medication.

We specialise in anxiety-driven insomnia

Not general relaxation, not sleep tracking — a structured, clinically-grounded path built specifically for the "exhausted but wired" pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MindVeda.

MindVeda is not meditation. We do not ask you to clear your mind, sit in silence, or listen to sounds. An overactive brain actually gets more frustrated trying to think about nothing at 1:00 AM. Instead of giving you a library to work through, it sends one focused exercise, prompt, or short audio each night through WhatsApp.
Less than 15 minutes a night. There are no long lectures, heavy modules, or 50-page PDFs to read. It is engineered to be completely frictionless. You simply open a single daily audio notification inside WhatsApp, complete the routine, log your progress, and go straight to sleep.
No. MindVeda is built 100% on WhatsApp, There are zero app store downloads, zero storage requirements on your phone, and zero new passwords to remember.
Across the 21 nights, you'll learn and practise practical sleep techniques for things like racing thoughts, lying awake, clock-checking, physical tension, nighttime wake-ups and rebuilding a healthier sleep routine. You'll receive the guidance directly through WhatsApp, with short lessons, simple actions and guided audio at the relevant points in the program.
Sleep problems don't have one single cause, and MindVeda isn't a guaranteed cure for every type of sleep difficulty. But if you don't see any visible improvements, we offer a full refund within 30 days. Our goal is to help you sleep better.
MindVeda is not a medical treatment or clinical therapy. It is an educational, structured sleep routine designed to help adults who feel exhausted but mentally switched on at night. It is not a replacement for professional medical care.
